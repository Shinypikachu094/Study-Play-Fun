# Project brief: Study, Play, Fun!

## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: One deadline is registration which has to be done by December 18 at 5:00 pm Eastern time and you have to submit your project by then. The second deadline is that you have to write an entry and upload a video of you explaining your project and you have to write up to 10 pages and record a 4 minute video.

## The problem
- People in school struggle with studying and remembering brainstorming ideas because they don't have motivation to study or don't have a neat system to keep track of notes and ideas.
- Impact area: School and learning
- Why it matters (impact): If my project works, people's lives would be better because they could study easily and remember brainstorming ideas from years ago.
- Evidence it’s real: I got facts from this site, https://ctli.vermontstate.edu/teaching-effectiveness-framework/instructional-strategies/note-taking-guidance/, that shows how students don't have the motivation to write notes down and to study.
- What people told me: Person A: I want to play video games instead of studying for the test
Person B: I'm too lazy to save the brainstorming ideas I have.

## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by giving students motivation and tips and helps them autosave their brainstorming ideas and automatically writes study goals. Which works better than students trying to find motivation themselves and having to write or type their brainstorming ideas because they could easily think of a lot of good ideas but only remember a few of them by the time they start typing.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: One tool is https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education and another is https://www.khanacademy.org/college-careers-more/khanmigo-for-students, my website is different than these tools because it is simpler for students who want quick motivation and automatic study goals.
- Safety plan: A lot of things could go wrong for my users like, asking medical questions and trusting them, and do not share private information, in order to prevent that add warnings that say do not share personal information, and AI can be wrong with medical issues.

## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: The user has to ask help for something educational related.
- Its job: My AI's job is helping users short brainstorming ideas and helping them study for tests.
- Who it talks to and how to sound: My AI is talking to people who need help saving ideas and studying and it should sound to them like an AI that is helpful and that understands what they actually want.
- Facts it must use: Some facts my AI will use is tips for good study habits and helping users tag and sort their ideas.
- Rules it must follow: My AI must never show personal information and should never save any privacy information.
- How to answer: My AI should start by asking the user what it could help them with today and then give them a short but useful answer when they ask questions.

## The game
- What the player does over and over: Move the catcher to collect falling idea bubbles while avoiding distractions.
- How you win or lose: Catch enough idea bubbles before time runs out. Missing bubbles costs lives; after the goal, ask the AI Coach for one short educational tip to finish the level.
